Kupidx Child Safety Standard

Kupidx, published by AM24, maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward the exploitation, abuse, or endangerment of anyone under the age of 18. This standard explains the safeguards that are built into the Kupidx experience and the behaviors that will result in immediate enforcement action.

Who Must Follow This Standard

Every person who creates an account on Kupidx, uses Kupidx services, or represents Kupidx (including moderators, contractors, and partners) must comply. The standard covers all content, messages, live interactions, and any offline activity coordinated through the app.

Age Gate and Eligibility

  • Kupidx accounts are available only to individuals who are 18 years of age or older.
  • Date of birth is collected during registration and access is blocked for anyone who cannot confirm they are at least 18.
  • We reserve the right to request additional verification when age is uncertain or reported by the community.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following activity is expressly forbidden on Kupidx. Attempts to engage in or facilitate any item on this list will lead to account termination and referral to the proper authorities:

  • Producing, possessing, or sharing any child sexual abuse or exploitation material (CSAE).
  • Soliciting sexual contact, explicit imagery, or personal details from anyone believed to be under 18.
  • Grooming or otherwise cultivating a relationship with a minor for the purpose of abuse or exploitation.
  • Sexualizing minors through text, imagery, audio, or video, including fetishization or fantasy role play involving minors.
  • Sextortion, blackmail, or coercion involving a minor’s intimate images.
  • Trafficking, recruitment, or advertising that targets minors for commercial sexual exploitation.
  • Any encouragement or facilitation of self-harm, dangerous stunts, or other harmful acts involving minors.

Our Safeguards

  • Automated detection models scan for grooming patterns, sexual content involving minors, and other high-risk signals.
  • 24/7 Trust & Safety coverage reviews urgent alerts, removes violative content, and escalates to law enforcement when required.
  • Restricted sharing: new accounts have limited reach until reputation and safety checks are passed.
  • Immediate account suspension for any credible report or evidence of child endangerment.

Tools for the Community

  • Use the in-app Report button on profiles, chats, or posts to flag suspected CSAE.
  • Block abusive accounts to cut off contact instantly while we investigate.
  • Email kupidxindia@gmail.com for urgent follow-up or to provide law enforcement case numbers.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement before anything else and then notify us so we can support the investigation.

Enforcement and Cooperation

Kupidx permanently removes accounts that violate this standard, deletes offending material, and blocks associated devices. We preserve evidence and work with child protection organizations and law enforcement agencies worldwide whenever we detect or are alerted to CSAE. False reports made in bad faith may result in enforcement action.

Continuous Improvement

We routinely audit moderation tools, train our teams on emerging CSAE threats, and update this standard as laws evolve. Suggestions can be sent to kupidxindia@gmail.com.
